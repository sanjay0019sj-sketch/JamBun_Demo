Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

This policy explains what information Friend Quiz collects and how it is used. By using the site you agree to it.

1. What we collect

  • The nickname you type and your quiz answers.
  • The nickname and score of friends who play a quiz.
  • The date and time a quiz or score was saved.

We do not ask for your email, phone number, address or any account details. Please do not use your full real name as a nickname.

2. How we use it

This information is used only to run the quiz: to check answers, show scores and display the leaderboard.

3. Where it is stored

Data is stored with Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore). The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Our service providers may also process technical data such as your IP address when you visit.

4. Browser storage

The site saves a small temporary note in your browser session (for example, whether you won or lost your last quiz) to show the right message. It is cleared when you close the tab.

5. Advertising and cookies

We may show ads using Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can manage or turn off personalised ads at Google Ad Settings. To learn how Google uses data from sites that use its services, see Google's partner sites page.

6. Third party services

We use Google Firebase, Google Fonts, Google AdSense and GitHub Pages. Each has its own privacy policy.

7. Children

This site is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

8. Deleting your data

To remove your quiz or scores, email us from the Contact page with your quiz link or nickname.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
