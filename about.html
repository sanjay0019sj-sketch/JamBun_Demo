About Us

Friend Quiz is a free quiz for friends. You answer a few questions about yourself, share your link, and your friends try to guess your answers. At the end you see who really knows you and who is a fake friend.

How it works

  • Create: enter your name and pick your answers to 10 questions.
  • Share: send your quiz link to friends on Instagram, WhatsApp or anywhere else.
  • Guess: your friends answer, see right or wrong after each question, and get a score.
  • Compare: the leaderboard shows everyone's score, and friends who score too low are marked OUT.

Who made this

This site is a student project, built for fun to bring friends together. It is free to use and does not need an account or a sign up.
